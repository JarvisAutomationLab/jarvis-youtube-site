Last updated: October 20, 2026

About JARVIS YouTube

JARVIS YouTube is a personal desktop application that helps its developer manage and upload original videos to YouTube.

Google and YouTube Data

With authorization, the application accesses YouTube account information and uploads videos using the YouTube Data API.

How Data Is Used

Authorized account data is used to identify the connected YouTube channel and perform requested YouTube operations.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the developer's computer and are not published on this website.

Data Sharing

JARVIS does not sell Google user data. Information is shared with Google and YouTube as necessary to perform authorized operations.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked at Google Account Connections . Local authorization credentials can also be deleted to disconnect the application.

Google API Services

Use of YouTube services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy .
